AI for malaria parasite detection

Digital Malaria Control for the Developing World · Kigali, Rwanda · Research Assistant

Malaria diagnosis still relies on a trained microscopist reading blood films by eye, and trained microscopists are scarce. This project builds AI tools that find and classify malaria parasites in slide images to support that work.

  • Validate and label parasites in blood-film images to build reliable training data.
  • Apply ML and computer-vision models to detect and classify parasites.
  • Research and iterate on existing models to improve them.
