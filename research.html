Research · Applied ML for health

Medical AI built for the data Africa actually has.

I work on computer vision for medical images collected in low-resource settings: brain MRI, blood-film microscopy and retinal scans. The common thread is getting reliable results from small, local datasets, and being honest about where strong baselines are enough.

Research themes

Four problems I have worked on.

Each theme maps to at least one paper, poster or active project below.

Segmentation · MRI

Brain tumour segmentation

How well do standard pipelines like nnU-Net hold up on limited Sub-Saharan glioma MRI data?

Detection · Microscopy

Blood-film diagnostics

YOLO and Vision Transformers for leukemia detection, and AI for malaria parasite detection and classification.

Sequence · Pose

Sign language recognition

Translating pose sequences directly to text for continuous sign language recognition.

Classification · Retina

Diabetic retinopathy screening

Automated detection of diabetic retinopathy from retinal images to support screening.
