Most of my work starts with a question from someone who is not a data person: a ministry planner, a programme manager, a clinician. I scope the question, check the data is fit for it, do the analysis, and hand back something they can use, usually a dashboard or a short briefing.

On the research side I care about models that work with the data Africa actually has: small MRI cohorts, microscope slides from local labs, and limited annotation budgets.

  • Stakeholder-first reporting for technical and non-technical audiences
  • Data quality, validation and documentation built into the workflow
  • Reproducible analysis with Python, SQL and version control
